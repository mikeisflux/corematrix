# Third-party models and environment maps

- `robot.glb` — "RobotExpressive" by Tomás Laulhé (Quaternius), CC0 1.0. Converted and
  modified by Don McCurdy for the three.js examples. https://quaternius.com
- `../hdri/warehouse.hdr` — "Empty Warehouse 01" from Poly Haven, CC0 1.0.
  https://polyhaven.com/a/empty_warehouse_01

- `avatar-a.glb`, `avatar-b.glb` — the visitors' bodies, faces, eyes, eyebrows and hair styles are the
  **Universal Base Characters** by Quaternius (CC0 1.0: the male and female full bodies and the hair and
  eyebrow pieces), taken from the OpenUSD copies in github.com/chibifire-stages/quaternius-stage.
  https://quaternius.com — The shirt, trousers and shoes are generated from those bodies by this project's
  `scripts/avatars/usd-to-glb.py`. The idle, walk, run, sit, cheer, bow and point animations are from
  **KayKit – Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0** by Kay Lousberg, CC0 1.0, retargeted onto that rig.
  https://kaylousberg.com — The costume parts, badge and lanyard, the wave/dance/clap/laugh/facepalm
  emotes and the assembly are this project's (`src/lib/hall/modelkit/`, `scripts/avatars/`).

Everything else in this folder (`table*.glb`, `chair.glb`, `banner_stand.glb`, `cabinet.glb`,
`light.glb`) is generated by `npm run models` from `src/lib/hall/modelkit/` and is part of this
project. Replace any of them with your own `.glb` that keeps the same node and material names and
the scene picks it up with no code changes.
