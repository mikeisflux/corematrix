# Third-party models and environment maps

- `robot.glb` — "RobotExpressive" by Tomás Laulhé (Quaternius), CC0 1.0. Converted and
  modified by Don McCurdy for the three.js examples. https://quaternius.com
- `../hdri/warehouse.hdr` — "Empty Warehouse 01" from Poly Haven, CC0 1.0.
  https://polyhaven.com/a/empty_warehouse_01

- `avatar-a.glb`, `avatar-b.glb` — the visitors. Bodies, skin, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, hair styles,
  T-shirts, jeans and shoes are **MakeHuman** assets (base mesh and targets, the young light-skinned
  skins, `male_casualsuit06`, `female_casualsuit01`, `shoes05`, the `short01`–`04`, `afro01`, `bob01`–`02`,
  `long01`, `ponytail01` and `braid01` hair, the low-poly eyes, `eyebrow001`, `eyelashes01`), all CC0 1.0.
  http://www.makehumancommunity.org — The skeleton and its skin weights are the "game engine" rig of
  **MPFB2** (MakeHuman Plugin For Blender), whose assets are CC0 1.0. https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2
  — Every animation (idle, walk, run, sit and the eight emotes) is motion capture from the **Carnegie Mellon
  University Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database**, free for research and commercial use, in the BVH
  conversion by Bruce Hahne (cgspeed.com): "The data used in this project was obtained from
  mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217." — The fitting, the
  retargeting, the costume parts, badge and lanyard and the assembly are this project's
  (`scripts/avatars/`, `src/lib/hall/modelkit/`).

Everything else in this folder (`table*.glb`, `chair.glb`, `banner_stand.glb`, `cabinet.glb`,
`light.glb`) is generated by `npm run models` from `src/lib/hall/modelkit/` and is part of this
project. Replace any of them with your own `.glb` that keeps the same node and material names and
the scene picks it up with no code changes.
